Cognitive Aging Research Lab — Demo Report

Study type: deterministic synthetic longitudinal cohort; five visits across eight years; no patient records.

Objective

Demonstrate a shared technical workflow for analyzing social determinants of cognitive aging and biological markers in longitudinal observational datasets.

Data provenance and quality

Social research workstream

Stratified synthetic cognitive trajectories are visualized for neighborhood deprivation groups. A causal estimand, an illustrative DAG and a future IPTW workflow are specified; no adjusted causal effect is estimated.

Biological research workstream

Three synthetic biomarker variables are explored using unadjusted Pearson associations with cognitive scores. These are demo variables, not medical measurements. Proposed next steps include adjusted models and multiple-testing controls.

Prediction protocol

A baseline-to-Year-8 prediction task is specified with participant-grouped evaluation. There are no trained model outputs, accuracy metrics or clinical claims in this release.

Reproducibility and limitations

Records are generated using a fixed seeded algorithm. Screens and exports derive from one data source. This artifact is intended to demonstrate application architecture and research workflow only. No real-world effect sizes, causal conclusions, biomarker validity or model generalization should be inferred.

Prototype v0.1 · Deterministic synthetic cohort · Not for clinical decisions · Research methods require validation before publication.
