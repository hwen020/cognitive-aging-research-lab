# NHANES statistical analysis plan

The exploratory portfolio analysis treats DSST as primary and the other scores as secondary. The choice is documented before fitting in R. Analyze education as five ordered categories entered as a factor with college graduate or above as reference. Enter poverty income ratio linearly in units of one ratio point. Adjust for age in years, recorded sex category, race and Hispanic-origin category as social descriptors, and survey cycle. Include HbA1c in percentage points and mean systolic blood pressure per 10 mm Hg in the biological model. DBP is harmonized for quality checks and excluded from the joint model because SBP and DBP share the same examination. These measures are general cardiometabolic indicators.

Construct the survey design in the examined sample with positive MEC weights before subsetting the age domain. Combine cycles with WTMEC2YR divided by two and retain masked PSU and strata. All included components use MEC weights and HbA1c does not require a fasting-subsample weight for these cycles. Fit linear survey regressions with design-based variance. Report 95% confidence intervals and apply BH within the social and biological exposure families across four outcomes. See [CDC weighting guidance](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/nchs/nhanes/tutorials/weighting.aspx) and the [CDC HbA1c codebook](https://wwwn.cdc.gov/Nchs/Data/Nhanes/Public/2013/DataFiles/GHB_H.htm).

Report complete-case counts and missing fractions by variable. Refit the social DSST model in the biological complete cases and age 70+ domain. For biological sensitivity, require at least two BP readings, fit unadjusted single-marker models, and perform a joint quadratic-term design test. Treat these as exploratory. Summarize residual RMSE and the correlation of the two clinical predictors.

Predict concurrent DSST in 2013–2014 using models trained solely in 2011–2012. Compare the training mean, social linear regression, augmented linear regression, and a fixed-complexity regression tree. Use the same complete cases for each model. Report unweighted test MAE, RMSE and R² for performance in this holdout sample. Contrast paired absolute errors of augmented versus social linear models using masked PSU resampling within holdout strata, conditional on fitted models. This evaluation does not support diagnosis or future cognitive decline prediction.
