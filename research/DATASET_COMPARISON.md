## Ranked candidates for the next project extension

Ranking weights scientific value beyond NHANES and VCAP, publicly documented fields, access burden and the effort to define a defensible analysis. NHANES and VCAP are reference analyses and are outside the candidate ranking. Access and usable sample sizes remain conditional.

1. [OASIS-2](datasets/oasis-2.md) — Best narrowly scoped imaging pilot; verify access and paired timing.
2. [NHATS](datasets/nhats.md) — Best next applied US social-aging extension; use cohort weights and proxy/death routes.
3. [ELSA](datasets/elsa.md) — Best complementary wealth and functioning panel; resolve licence and wave choices.
4. [OASIS-3](datasets/oasis-3.md) — Best deeper imaging extension after a small pilot; higher integration burden.
5. [MIDUS](datasets/midus.md) — Strong psychosocial extension; biomarker module overlap remains uncertain.
6. [UAS CogCF](datasets/uas-cogcf.md) — Useful recent web-panel evidence; pin instruments and terms.
7. [NACC UDS](datasets/nacc.md) — Clinical progression value, conditional on approved UDS freeze and endpoint harmonization.
8. [ADNI](datasets/adni.md) — High biomarker value but governed workflow and phase-specific alignment increase cost.
9. [HRS / RAND HRS](datasets/hrs.md) — High social scientific value; authorized human-only empirical workflow under current policy.

OASIS-2 ranks first for imaging with a narrow derivatives-only question. NHATS ranks first for a new U.S. social-aging design. Neither ranking implies new access or empirical findings.
