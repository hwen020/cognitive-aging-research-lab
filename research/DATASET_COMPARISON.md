# Dataset comparison

| Dataset | Population, time and repeat structure | Measures and appropriate use | Interpretation limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| [NHANES](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-aging-data/nhanes/index.html) | Independent U.S. survey samples in 2011–2012 and 2013–2014. Age 60+ cognitive domain. SEQN joins survey components within a cycle. | DSST, CERAD, fluency, education, income, measured HbA1c and blood pressure. Complex-survey cross-sectional associations. | No within-person change. Cross-cycle prediction targets contemporaneous performance. |
| [VCAP](https://doi.org/10.18130/V3/QRCQ08) | UVA catalog describes an adult volunteer cohort, 2001–2019, with repeated participant observations. Exact time fields and overlap require audit. | Cognitive test trajectories; demographic and non-cognitive measures pending dictionary and file verification. | No assumed MRI, blood biomarker or diagnosis data. Practice and selection require analysis. |
| [OASIS-2](https://sites.wustl.edu/oasisbrains/) | Planned longitudinal MRI extension. Confirm dates, visit structure and MRI-to-clinical linkage during later feasibility review. | Candidate structural MRI derivatives and clinical outcomes, subject to field and access verification. | Clinical relevance and usable sample sizes remain unassessed. |
| [UAS CogCF](https://uasdata.usc.edu/index.php?pid=Cognitive+Comprehensive+File&type=3) | USC U.S. probability internet panel. Multiple cognitive waves, completion dates and stable respondent ID. | Cognitive and ability scores linked to eligible education, economic and health surveys. | Harmonize instruments and selection over waves before interpreting change. |
| [HRS](https://hrsdata.isr.umich.edu/data-products) | U.S. aging panel with repeated waves. Pin product and release in the authorized environment. | Cognitive performance, education, wealth, income and health. Framework supports person-repeated models. | AI tools may not handle HRS microdata. |
| [NACC UDS](https://www.naccdata.org/data-front-door) | ADRC cohort, standardized annual evaluations beginning in 2005. Participant linkage and coverage depend on requested releases. | Cognitive and functional assessments and diagnoses. MRI and biomarkers require separately verified products and linkages. | ADRC cohort is not a probability sample of the general population. |
