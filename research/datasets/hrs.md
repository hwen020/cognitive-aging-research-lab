## Verified Dataset Characteristics

**Review status** — Metadata Only · FRAMEWORK_ONLY. Public sources reviewed 2026-11-24.

### Overview, population and structure

U.S. aging survey with repeated interviews. The RAND HRS 1992–2022 V1 documentation describes harmonized respondent and wave records and cognition modes.

### Cognitive, social, biological and imaging measures

RAND documentation verifies immediate/delayed recall, serial sevens and RwCOG27 (wave 3 onward with mode adjustment from wave 14), plus education, income, wealth and self-reported health. Separate biomarkers require release-specific authorization and field verification; MRI for this design: Not Verified.

### Strengths, limitations and added value

Adds wealth and later-life health trajectories to VCAP and temporal structure to NHANES. Proxy responses, mode change, mortality and imputation may change the estimand.

### Access and governance

Public documentation is reviewable; files require registration and conditions. HRS explicitly prohibits AI processing of person-level HRS data, including RAND releases. A human-operated approved environment is required for any future empirical execution.

## Proposed Research Design

**Testable question.** Among self-interviewed age-50+ HRS respondents in comparable waves, is baseline household wealth associated with later total word recall after age, sex, education and baseline self-reported health adjustment?

**Design, analysis and limitations.** Use elapsed follow-up and wealth-by-time with survey-aware estimates, comparing observed and documented imputed cognitive measures and handling proxy and death separately. Prior cognition may affect wealth, and attrition may preferentially remove poorer or less healthy respondents.

**Social and biological suitability.** Scientifically strong social benchmark; no AI-assisted microdata execution under current policy.

## Completed Empirical Research

This profile is a public-source evaluation. The proposed design has no project sample estimate or model result.
