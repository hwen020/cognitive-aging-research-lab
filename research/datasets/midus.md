## Verified Dataset Characteristics

**Review status** — Metadata Only. Public sources reviewed 2026-11-24.

### Overview, population and structure

U.S. adult longitudinal program with original national, family and regional samples and a separate refresher. Cognition and biomarker projects require respondent linkage and participation tracking; releases are project-specific.

### Cognitive, social, biological and imaging measures

MIDUS 2 BTACT notes verify immediate unique word recall B3TWLITU and backward span B3TDBS. ICPSR series documents cognitive waves and psychosocial survey content; education is a candidate social measure. The MIDUS 2 biomarker project documents glycosylated hemoglobin and assay changes. Linked repeat coverage with BTACT is Not Verified. [Laboratory documentation](https://midus-study.github.io/public-documentation/M2P4/Documentation/M2_P4_Blood-Urine-Saliva-Data_Documentation_20240423.pdf). Imaging is separately restricted.

### Strengths, limitations and added value

Adds psychosocial context and possible biological linkage missing from VCAP, and repeat cognition beyond NHANES. Long intervals, family clustering, telephone mode, refresher structure and biomarker-substudy selection matter.

### Access and governance

Use official archive terms. MIDUS explicitly prohibits redistributing obtained data and describes restricted imaging/genomics repositories. AI permission Not Explicitly Specified in reviewed MIDUS policy. Individual biomarker catalog endpoint returned HTTP 429 during review.

## Proposed Research Design

**Testable question.** Among original national-core participants with BTACT follow-up, is education associated with change in immediate recall between MIDUS 2 and 3 after accounting for baseline age and sex?

**Design, analysis and limitations.** Model recall on elapsed time and education-by-time, cluster by respondent and family where applicable, and compare survey-design estimates with attrition-weighted sensitivity. Control age, sex and available early-life socioeconomic indicators once mapped. Initial education selection and differential telephone participation limit interpretation.

**Social and biological suitability.** Strong for social research; biological expansion conditional on separate linkage and verified assay coverage.

## Completed Empirical Research

This profile is a public-source evaluation. The proposed design has no project sample estimate or model result.
