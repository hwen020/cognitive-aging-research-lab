## Verified Dataset Characteristics

**Review status** — Metadata Only. Public sources reviewed 2026-11-24.

### Overview, population and structure

U.S. Medicare beneficiaries aged 65+, overlapping replenishment cohorts and annual rounds beginning in 2011. The sample-person and Tracker documentation distinguishes interview, proxy, and vital status paths.

### Cognitive, social, biological and imaging measures

Public user guide documents immediate and delayed word recall, clock drawing (cg#dclkdraw) and proxy-related codes. Functional assistance and living arrangements are survey domains. The official methods index documents separate dried blood spot biomarkers. Cognition-to-biospecimen overlap and MRI linkage remain Not Verified. Clock drawings are task records.

### Strengths, limitations and added value

Adds representative later-life function and proxies beyond VCAP and time ordering beyond NHANES. Mortality, proxy transition and differences across cohorts or collection modes are salient.

### Access and governance

Public-use files require registration; sensitive files require application. Public-use conditions prohibit uploads to public AI platforms and limit transfer to supervised staff or students; other collaborators need separate access. Aggregate reporting and citation are required. [Official conditions](https://www.nhats.org/data-access).

## Proposed Research Design

**Testable question.** Among baseline self-interviewed beneficiaries, does living alone predict poorer later immediate recall after age, sex, education, baseline recall and baseline limitations are accounted for?

**Design, analysis and limitations.** Use a defined inception cohort and compatible longitudinal weight; estimate among survivors with follow-up, contrasting inverse-probability observation weighting and a proxy/competing mortality sensitivity. Health and support may confound; proxy respondents cannot be coded as low test scores.

**Social and biological suitability.** High social and gerontological feasibility after registered access; biological inference limited to verified measured domains.

## Completed Empirical Research

This profile is a public-source evaluation. The proposed design has no project sample estimate or model result.
