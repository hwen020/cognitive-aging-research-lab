## Verified Dataset Characteristics

**Review status** — Metadata Only. Public sources reviewed 2026-11-24.

### Overview, population and structure

England's older-adult panel recruited from earlier health surveys with refreshment samples. UKDS SN 5050 covers waves 0–11, 1998–2024; core interview and health visits have different coverage.

### Cognitive, social, biological and imaging measures

Wave 5 derived variables guide verifies immediate/delayed word recall and derived cognitive indices. Official financial guides document derived wealth measures; health-visit documentation confirms blood collection and biomarkers. Specific HbA1c field and paired-wave coverage: Not Verified in this review. MRI availability for this design: Not Verified.

### Strengths, limitations and added value

Adds wealth trajectories, aging-panel coverage and nurse-measured health beyond both benchmarks. Wave replenishment, survey response and health-visit selection must be accounted for.

### Access and governance

UKDS licensed access; linked/genetic data have separate access. UKDS security guidance prohibits consumer AI uploads of individual records. Fully local tools are considered separately under the EUL; any external or institutional AI use must be assessed under the applicable terms.

## Proposed Research Design

**Testable question.** Among core ELSA members aged 50+ at a chosen entry wave, are lower baseline wealth and time associated with steeper decline in delayed word recall, controlling age, sex, education and baseline health?

**Design, analysis and limitations.** Select common wave instrument, inflation-adjust the financial measure, fit wave/elapsed-time by wealth with random participant effects and assess design/attrition weights. Early-life disadvantage confounds and cognition may affect wealth; restrict a lagged analysis and report differential dropout.

**Social and biological suitability.** High social value, potential biological extension after nurse linkage; medium feasibility due to licensed access and weights.

## Completed Empirical Research

This profile is a public-source evaluation. The proposed design has no project sample estimate or model result.
