## Verified Dataset Characteristics

**Review status** — Metadata Only. Public sources reviewed 2026-11-24.

### Overview, population and structure

Retrospective Knight ADRC compilation. Imaging dictionary v2.3, release 2.0, reports 1,378 participants over roughly 30 years of source projects. Subject IDs plus days from entry link uneven visits and modalities; coverage varies.

### Cognitive, social, biological and imaging measures

Dictionary verifies clinical CDR, T1 MRI with available FreeSurfer outputs, and tracer-specific PET including amyloid imaging. Age and sex are documented. Individual education, income and assay coverage for the proposed subset: Not Verified.

### Strengths, limitations and added value

Adds regional morphometry and multimodal timing beyond VCAP and NHANES. Valuable clinical and imaging depth, with scanner, processing, tracer and visit-timing heterogeneity and uncertain concurrent coverage.

### Access and governance

Access Restricted. WashU application and terms plus NITRC account are documented. Release-specific restrictions on further disclosure apply. AI permission Not Explicitly Specified in reviewed terms.

## Proposed Research Design

**Testable question.** Among participants with baseline T1-derived hippocampal volume and later clinical assessments, is smaller baseline volume associated with faster worsening in scored global CDR?

**Design, analysis and limitations.** Define a clinical timing window anchored to baseline MRI; fit an ordinal mixed model with volume-by-time, age, sex, eTIV or equivalent verified head-size measure, and scanner/pipeline indicators. Repeat with a narrower window. Selection into imaging and clinical follow-up, diagnostic spectrum and scanner changes can bias estimates.

**Social and biological suitability.** High biological added value but moderate feasibility; require derivatives, head-size field and repeated outcome overlap before committing.

## Completed Empirical Research

This profile is a public-source evaluation. The proposed design has no project sample estimate or model result.
