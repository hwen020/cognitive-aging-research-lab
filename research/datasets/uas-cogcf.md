## Verified Dataset Characteristics

**Review status** — Metadata Only. Public sources reviewed 2026-11-24.

### Overview, population and structure

USC probability-based internet panel. Multiple cognitive series and waves, with stable uasid, completion dates and response indicators. Pin an extract because the product is updated frequently.

### Cognitive, social, biological and imaging measures

Official CogCF description verifies word recall in later UAS HRS-series waves, serial sevens, self-rated memory, and online cognitive tasks. Education and financial/health variables require documented linkage to companion files. Measured biomarkers and imaging in CogCF: Not Verified.

### Strengths, limitations and added value

Adds recent web-administered repeated cognition and linkable social measures. Mode, repeated exposure to tests, survey engagement, shifting instruments and internet context affect comparability.

### Access and governance

Registration and an individual DUA are required. UAS prohibits sharing microdata with generative AI and requires collaborators to have separate accounts and DUAs. [UAS data management policy](https://uasdata.usc.edu/index.php?pid=Data+Management+Policies+and+Services&type=3).

## Proposed Research Design

**Testable question.** Among adult panelists with comparable later-series word recall, does baseline education predict change in immediate recall across dated waves after adjusting for age, sex and baseline survey engagement?

**Design, analysis and limitations.** Fit weighted mixed models with time-by-education and wave/mode indicators; assess respondent nonresponse and practice sensitivity using repeat-exposure count where verified. Test mode and engagement may be associated with education; repeat participants may not represent the whole panel.

**Social and biological suitability.** High social value if linkage and terms are confirmed; limited biological scope without separate verified measures.

## Completed Empirical Research

This profile is a public-source evaluation. The proposed design has no project sample estimate or model result.
