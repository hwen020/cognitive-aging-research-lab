## Verified Dataset Characteristics

**Review status** — Completed Empirical Research. Public sources reviewed 2026-11-24.

### Overview, population and structure

UVA volunteer adult cohort, 2001–2019, with uneven repeat occasions. Three sessions within an occasion form a measurement burst and are distinct from a longitudinal return.

### Cognitive, social, biological and imaging measures

The documented workbook keys are SubjID and Occasion. Session 1 date anchors elapsed time. DigSym1 Score counts correct matches in 120 seconds; Age, Educ and recorded Sex come from Demographics. Word recall and other cognitive measures are documented. MRI or biomarker linkage in V1.0: Not Verified.

### Strengths, limitations and added value

Broad adulthood coverage and dated visits are strengths. Retest effects, volunteer recruitment and selective return weaken population inference. Adds real cognitive trajectories beyond NHANES, and is the existing non-imaging longitudinal benchmark.

### Access and governance

CC0 catalog label plus guestbook, research-only and no identification expectations, secure storage and citation. Dataset-specific AI terms: Not Explicitly Specified in the cited catalog. This review used metadata and the existing published audit.

## Proposed Research Design

**Testable question.** Among baseline-eligible VCAP adults, is education associated with Digit Symbol slope after adjusting for baseline age, age-by-time and recorded sex?

**Design, analysis and limitations.** Use actual elapsed years in a mixed-effects model with education-by-time and participant random intercept/slope. Compare first-retest adjustment and repeat-only fits. Cohort effects, unmeasured socioeconomic differences and selective return limit interpretation; education is not randomized.

**Social and biological suitability.** High for cognitive measurement and education; limited for biomarker questions without a verified linkage.
