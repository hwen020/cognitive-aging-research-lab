# Research gaps and limitations

[Research index](README.md) · [Access and governance](DATA_ACCESS_AND_GOVERNANCE.md)

- **Cross-sectional versus longitudinal.** NHANES cross-cycle prediction targets contemporaneous performance in independent samples. VCAP can estimate observed within-person change, but repeat attendees can differ from baseline participants.
- **Outcome harmonization.** Digit Symbol, immediate recall, global CDR and MoCA measure different constructs. Compare directions within a construct or validate a harmonization method; do not pool their raw scores.
- **Social-to-biological linkage.** Publicly documented biomarker products do not establish shared respondents or visits. MIDUS, ELSA, OASIS-3, ADNI and NACC need a versioned overlap audit before cross-modal modeling.
- **Attrition and mode.** In NHATS and HRS, proxy interviews and death are substantive follow-up states. In MIDUS, ELSA and UAS, design, refreshment and mode can distort apparent trajectories.
- **Image timing and selection.** OASIS and ADNI participants can have nonconcurrent clinical and scan sessions. Define time zero, prespecify windows and examine who is excluded at each stage.
- **Access and evidence.** Not Verified means a field or policy was not established for the proposed release. Not Explicitly Specified means the reviewed public terms did not address the question directly. Catalog counts are not analytic denominators or precision estimates. No row-level sample audit was done in this review.
