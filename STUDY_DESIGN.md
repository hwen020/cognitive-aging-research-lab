# Study design

NHANES is a pooled repeated cross-section. The target domain is MEC-examined adults aged 60 and older in the U.S. civilian noninstitutionalized population represented by these cycles. Analyze observed cognitive test performance. Test completion and covariate missingness can change the effective target of complete-case models. Cycle is a period indicator, and masked PSUs and strata are nested within cycle for variance estimation. SEQN keys link components within cycle only.

Primary outcome is the Digit Symbol Substitution Test (DSST), measured in correct assignments. Secondary outcomes are the sum of three CERAD immediate recall trials, CERAD delayed recall and animal fluency. HRS is a separate longitudinal protocol pending independent authorized execution.
