# Limits of interpretation

- The generator dictates relationships and visit patterns. Synthetic markers are proxies in simulated units and the cognition score has no validated clinical meaning.
- The social mixed-effects slope is conditional on the specified fixed covariates and residual covariance structure. Conditional lag-one residual ACF is included so readers can assess sensitivity of the working independence assumption.
- IPTW uses a simulator-assigned exposure. The incomplete-mode contrast is left unestimated. Real-world exchangeability, positivity, exposure definition, selection, censoring and measurement need independent assessment.
- The biological models use marker-specific complete rows, requiring a baseline measurement. Approaches for selection or informative missingness would need a separate prespecified analysis.
- Prediction reports uncertainty conditional on the fitted holdout predictions. It has no external validation. Incomplete-mode metrics condition on observing Year-8 cognition. Feature importance and subgroup summaries are descriptive.

## Public NHANES module

Pooling independent surveys describes 2011–2014 cross-sectional performance and cannot measure an individual's decline, predict their future score, or estimate a causal effect. The design weights address survey sampling and MEC nonresponse, while remaining item nonresponse and cognitive test completion can bias complete-case estimates. Adjustment does not eliminate residual confounding or measurement differences. The held-out survey cycle evaluates concurrent prediction. Its small paired difference is conditional on the fitted models, complete cases, and literature-informed variable choices. No external clinical validation was performed.
